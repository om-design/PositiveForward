# Positive Forward

**The science of establishing reliable understanding across diverse intelligence systems**

Originator: omdesign  
Foundational statement: 26 November 2026  
Status: Proposed research program

## Concept

Positive Forward is a research initiative concerned with establishing reliable understanding between intelligence systems whose foundations, experience, and methods of reasoning may differ or be unknown to one another.

Its central premise is that understanding cannot be assumed from fluent communication, agreement, or familiarity with terminology. An intelligence system may reason coherently from premises that differ substantially from those intended by the originator of an idea.

## Origin and purpose

Positive Forward arose during the development of Has-Needs, when independent AI systems repeatedly interpreted unfamiliar architectural concepts through familiar technological and social paradigms. Even when supplied with documents intended to prevent these substitutions, models could interpret the guidance itself through an existing paradigm.

The resulting divergence is consequential. A model can construct a technically competent implementation, conduct a rigorous experiment, and produce reproducible results that describe a different mechanism from the one under investigation. Inadequate specifications can contribute equally to this problem.

AI systems differ in their training, architecture, context, and behavior. Even successive versions of the same system cannot be assumed to interpret information consistently. Positive Forward therefore seeks to establish reliable understanding through the design of the interaction itself, rather than depending on an intelligence system's ability to recognize or correct its own assumptions.

## Research direction

The proposed methodology begins by identifying foundational concepts that can be independently verified, then arranging information so that each successive concept follows from those already established. Comprehension is tested through the application of those concepts to unfamiliar situations, rather than through repetition or agreement. Observed divergences and corrections are preserved outside the participating intelligence system.

Research will compare approaches across models, platforms, contexts, and time. It will investigate how terminology, foundational analogies, specification quality, constructive sequencing, and comprehension checks influence the fidelity of interpretation. Foundational concepts themselves, including mathematical and physical reasoning, are subject to verification rather than presumed reliable.

The initial research cases arise from [Has-Needs](https://github.com/has-needs/docs) and [Jitterbug Network Topology](https://github.com/om-design/JitterbugNetworkTopology). The research program is independent of either project and is intended to contribute to scientific inquiry, education, engineering, and collaboration among diverse intelligence systems.

Positive Forward is a proposed field of investigation, not a claim that reliable understanding has already been achieved. Its methods and effectiveness remain subject to independent evaluation.
